A solo superhero adventure using the FASERIP rules

FASERIP: Blackout

The universal chart

Every feat is a roll of 1 to 100 read against the column for the rank being used.

How the rules work

Everything is measured on a scale of named ranks, each with a value: Average is 6, World Class is 30, Fantastic is 40. Your hero has seven abilities: Fighting, Agility, Strength, Endurance, Reason, Intuition and Psyche. Health is the values of the first four added together. Karma starts as the last three added together.

To attempt a feat, roll 1 to 100 and read the column for your rank. White fails. Bronze just succeeds, Silver succeeds with room to spare, and Gold succeeds with ease. A rank shift (RS) moves you a column left or right before you roll.

A ranked feat is set against a difficulty rank. Two or more ranks above it succeeds without a roll; one above needs Bronze; equal needs Silver; one below needs Gold; lower than that cannot succeed.

In a fight, a hit does its damage rank's value, less the target's armour. Silver and Gold hits can also slam, stun or kill, which the target resists with a feat of Strength or Endurance. Lethal attacks that drop a foe leave them dying. Killing costs a hero all their karma, so give first aid.

Before a roll you can spend karma to guarantee a result. You pay the gap between your roll and the number you need, with a minimum of 10. In a fight you can also leave karma on guard, so it is spent to keep you conscious when a stun or killing blow would otherwise knock you out.
